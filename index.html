Weighted rankings

Weighted score = latest Power of One score, average of all loaded reports, and trend (points per month mapped to 0–100: flat = 50, +10/month = 100, −10/month = 0; under 3 months = 50). Only current members are ranked – within the region you pick, or across everything loaded. Chapters are ranked by their members' average. ▲▼ = places moved since the previous report.

Chapters

  • Each report covers the 6 months of PALMS data before it (the October report covers April–September).
  • Improving = trend ≥ +2 pts/month or ≥ +15 since first report; Declining = ≤ −2 pts/month or ≤ −15; otherwise Steady. Needs 3+ months.
  • Focus area = the category with the fewest points in the member's latest report.
  • Members are matched by name across chapters, so chapter moves keep their history. A spelling change shows as two members.
